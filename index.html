About the Conference

Background

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence, particularly with the rise of large language models, is fundamentally transforming the global workforce. This shift necessitates a new approach that puts people at the center of technological change.

Rationale

Focusing on human-centric AI ensures that the future of work is not just efficient, but also equitable, meaningful, and sustainable. By prioritizing human values, we mitigate risks like algorithmic bias and data privacy violations.

Objectives

  • Explore how human-centric AI can transform business strategies and models.
  • Share ideas on aligning AI adoption with ethics, inclusivity, and sustainability.
  • Discuss workforce transformation and human-AI collaboration.
  • Build bridges between academia and industry for responsible AI.
  • Provide recommendations for AI governance in business contexts.

🗓️ Program Structure

The conference will run over two days, including keynote talks, plenary sessions, and paper presentations. Day 1 will feature opening sessions and keynotes, while Day 2 will include plenaries and a valedictory session.

Expected Outcomes

Publication of Quality Research

A collection of papers and presentations that will serve as a valuable resource for future research and policy-making.

Human-Centric AI Toolkit

A practical guide for businesses on how to responsibly implement AI, with a better understanding of opportunities and risks.

Ethical AI Adoption

Presentations and discussions will lead to practical recommendations for ethical AI adoption in business.

Academic-Industry Working Group

A collaborative group to continue the dialogue and research, strengthening collaborations between all stakeholders.

Policy Recommendations

A set of recommendations for policymakers on how to foster a human-centric approach to AI development and regulation in India.

👥 Participants

The conference will bring together academics, researchers, industry leaders, entrepreneurs, policymakers, regulators, civil society groups, and students.

🤝 Collaborations and Partnerships

We have a collaboration with Institute Mines – Telecom Business School, France. We will seek partnerships with universities, AI research centers, businesses, startups, and government agencies.

📚 Publication and Dissemination

High-quality submissions may be recommended for journal publication. A post-conference white paper with policy recommendations will be released.

About Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV)

Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), also known as Devi Ahilya University, is a prominent public university located in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. Established in 1964, the university was initially known as the University of Indore and later renamed in honor of the legendary queen of the Maratha Empire, Devi Ahilya Bai Holkar, known for her contributions to society, art, and architecture.

DAVV offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs across disciplines like science, arts, commerce, law, management, engineering, education, and social sciences. The university has numerous academic departments and affiliated colleges that cater to different fields of study, including technology, health sciences, and professional courses.

About International Institute of Professional Studies (IIPS)

The International Institute of Professional Studies (IIPS) is a renowned academic institution under Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), Indore. Established in 1992, IIPS is a pioneer institute to provide a new dimension to professional education. IIPS is a realm where education meets innovation, and dreams take flight.

The Institute has been providing exceptional opportunities to students for the past 32 years. IIPS is known for its integrated and professional courses in the fields of management, computer science, and commerce. The institute has gained a reputation as a center of excellence, particularly for offering five-year integrated programs, which are designed to provide students with comprehensive education and professional training.
