Submission Guidelines

Please follow these instructions to ensure your research work meets the conference requirements.

Paper Format

Manuscripts must be submitted in PDF format following the standard IEEE conference template. Use 10pt Times New Roman font for the body text.

Length

Full papers should be between 6,000 to 8,000 words, including references and appendices. Abstracts should be approximately 500 words.

Review Process

All submissions undergo a double-blind peer-review process. At least two experts in the field will evaluate each paper for quality and relevance.

Originality Requirements

Only original, unpublished work will be considered. Plagiarism (including self-plagiarism) checks will be conducted on all submissions.
